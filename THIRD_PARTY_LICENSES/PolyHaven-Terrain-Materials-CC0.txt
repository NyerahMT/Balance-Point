Balance Point uses downsampled derivatives of the following Poly Haven texture assets in mobile builds:

- Sparse Grass — https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass
  Author: Amal Kumar
- Dirt — https://polyhaven.com/a/dirt
  Author: Charlotte Baglioni
- Rocks Ground 05 — https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_05
  Author: Rob Tuytel

The build stages 1K diffuse maps for all three materials and the OpenGL normal map from Dirt, then downsamples them for mobile use.
